About the school

A government school committed to excellence and equity.

Government Secondary School, Mota Kalavad is a trusted institution serving the educational needs of students in Standard 9 and Standard 10 under the GSHSEB curriculum. Our mission is to offer quality secondary education rooted in discipline, innovation, and community values.

The school is dedicated to creating a supportive learning environment where students gain both academic strength and personal confidence. From digital learning tools to guided board-exam preparation, every initiative is built around student success.
